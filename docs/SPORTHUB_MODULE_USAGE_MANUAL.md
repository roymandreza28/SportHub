**USER MANUAL DURING DEVELOPMENT AND MODULE USAGE FOR MODULES**

1. **Access and Authentication**

1.1. **Account Creation**

- **Player and Coach:** Create an account using a valid email address and personal details.
- **Coach accounts:** Must submit proof of coaching eligibility during registration.
- **Venue Facilitator:** Account is created by the system admin or assigned by the platform owner.
- **Organizer and Staff Roles:** Created and assigned by the admin or main organizer depending on system configuration.
- **Admin:** Created by the system owner or a higher-level administrator.

1.2. **Login Procedure**

- Open your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Opera, or Brave).
- Navigate to the SportHub application URL.
- Click the Login button.
- Enter your registered email and password.
- Click Submit to access the system.
- If the account is pending verification, the user will be notified and restricted until verification is completed.

1.3. **Role-Based Access**

- **Player:** Access player dashboard, matchmaking, tournaments, venues, profile, social features, and news feed.
- **Coach:** Access coach dashboard, player evaluation, matchmaking, venue registration, tournaments, social features, and coaching tools.
- **Venue Facilitator:** Manage allocated venues, bookings, schedules, news, facility operations, and tournament coordination.
- **Main Organizer:** Create tournaments, assign support roles, manage brackets, and oversee event operations.
- **Venue Organizer:** Manage venue-side match operations, score updates, and assigned tournament responsibilities.
- **Livestream Organizer:** Manage live streaming setup, event broadcast controls, and communication during active matches.
- **Admin:** Manage users, verify accounts, control roles, review inquiries, and monitor platform activities.

2. **Admin Module**

2.1. **User Management**

- Add, update, or deactivate user accounts.
- Assign or modify user roles such as player, coach, venue facilitator, organizer, venue organizer, livestream organizer, and admin.
- Review account verification status.
- Reset user access or support reactivation when necessary.

2.2. **Verification and Approval**

- Review uploaded proof of address and coach eligibility documents.
- Approve or reject user verification requests.
- Ensure accounts are marked as verified before full feature access is granted.
- Manage pending user accounts and support deactivation procedures if necessary.

2.3. **Monitoring and System Control**

- View platform statistics and dashboard metrics.
- Monitor user activity and system logs.
- Review support inquiries submitted by users.
- Check audit logs for important administrative actions.
- Maintain overall security and platform integrity.

3. **Player Module**

3.1. **Dashboard**

- View personal dashboard and account summary.
- Check active registrations, sport-related activity, profile information, and social updates.
- Access available tournaments, venues, and matchmaking opportunities.

3.2. **Profile and Social Features**

- Update personal profile details.
- Manage friendships and social connections.
- Send or receive messages through the system chat and conversation tools.
- Create and share posts, interact with the news feed, and react to published content.

3.3. **Tournament and Matchmaking**

- Register for tournaments and competitions.
- View personal tournament participation status.
- Create matchmaking requests for sport activities.
- Join or be matched with suitable players based on preferences and skill eligibility.

3.4. **Venue Registration**

- Search and book sport venues.
- Submit venue registrations for scheduled activities.
- Track registration status such as pending, approved, or rejected.

3.5. **News and Engagement**

- Read published news and announcements.
- React to content and leave comments where access is allowed.
- Stay informed about tournaments, events, and venue updates.

4. **Coach Module**

4.1. **Coach Dashboard**

- View account status, coverage, and assigned responsibilities.
- Access player-focused operations and evaluation tools.
- Monitor upcoming events, bookings, and tournament activities.

4.2. **Player Evaluation**

- Evaluate player skill levels using sport-specific assessment data.
- Record evaluation notes and performance details.
- Maintain coaching records for future player development decisions.

4.3. **Tournament and Team Management**

- Register players or teams in tournaments.
- Manage team composition and coordination.
- Create or update matchmaking requests for team participation.

4.4. **Venue Booking and Operations**

- Book available venues for training or scheduled activities.
- Coordinate venue usage and registrations.
- Manage available sports bookings tied to the coach’s registered activities.

4.5. **Social and Communication**

- Communicate with players and other system users.
- Share posts and interact with the platform news feed.
- Manage social connections and collaborative activities.

5. **Venue Facilitator Module**

5.1. **Facility Management**

- Add, update, and remove venue information.
- Manage courts, equipment, amenities, and operational details.
- Update venue schedule and booking availability.

5.2. **Booking and Approval**

- Review incoming venue registrations.
- Approve, reject, or manage booking requests tied to assigned venues.
- Monitor facility usage to reduce conflicts or duplication.

5.3. **News and Event Updates**

- Publish updates related to venue activities.
- Share operational announcements, event reminders, and important notices.

5.4. **Tournament Coordination**

- Create and manage tournaments associated with owned or assigned facilities.
- Coordinate venue-based tournament setup and scheduling.
- Manage tournament operational readiness before event start.

6. **Organizer Module**

6.1. **Main Organizer**

6.1.1. **Tournament Management**

- Create tournaments, define schedule, and specify venue and format.
- Set tournament status such as draft, open, active, completed, or cancelled.
- Manage registration rules and track participant confirmations.

6.1.2. **Role Assignment**

- Assign venue organizers to manage venue-side operations.
- Assign livestream organizers to handle broadcast operations.
- Coordinate responsibilities across multiple support roles.

6.1.3. **Bracket and Match Operations**

- Generate competition brackets after registration is complete.
- Update match outcomes and final results.
- Track match progression across rounds and stages.

6.1.4. **Livestream and News Management**

- Configure livestreams associated with events.
- Create announcements and operational news for the tournament.
- Coordinate event communication and public updates.

6.2. **Venue Organizer**

- Manage assigned match schedules and venue operations.
- Update scoreboard information, timeouts, and score changes during live matches.
- Monitor event progress on the venue side.
- Ensure recorded match activities are accurate and synchronized with tournament records.

6.3. **Livestream Organizer**

- Configure livestream titles, stream platform, and channel information.
- Start and end live streams.
- Manage livestream status and related communications.
- Moderate chat and monitor active stream operations.

7. **Operational Procedure**

7.1. **Daily Workflow**

7.1.1. **Players**

- Log in to the system.
- Check upcoming tournaments, venues, and matchmaking requests.
- Register for activities or complete profile updates.
- Interact with the news feed, social tools, and personal dashboard.

7.1.2. **Coaches**

- Log in and review player evaluation and match planning tasks.
- Update team or player registrations.
- Coordinate venue usage and tournament readiness.
- Monitor player progress and match-related performance.

7.1.3. **Venue Facilitators**

- Log in and review venue operations.
- Confirm booking requests and facility schedules.
- Monitor venue updates and manage event readiness.

7.1.4. **Main Organizers**

- Log in and review event pipeline.
- Create or modify tournaments.
- Assign staff and manage live event operations.
- Monitor results, bracket activity, and updates.

7.1.5. **Venue Organizers**

- Log in and access assigned matches.
- Record scores and manage live event updates.
- Work closely with main organizer and event staff.

7.1.6. **Livestream Organizers**

- Log in and review active or upcoming streams.
- Start broadcasts when the event is ready.
- Manage communication and ongoing stream controls.

7.1.7. **Administrators**

- Log in and review platform status.
- Approve pending accounts and manage verification tasks.
- Track inquiry records and system audit logs.
- Maintain platform integrity and settings.

7.2. **Data Entry and Tracking**

- User profiles, tournament entries, match results, venue reservations, and news data are stored in the database.
- Media such as profile images, proof uploads, and venue-related files are stored in system public storage.
- Progress updates and operational logs are maintained for tracking and auditing.
- Notifications and approvals are recorded to support administrative review.

8. **Maintenance and Support**

8.1. **System Updates**

- Regular updates should be applied to the frontend and backend to maintain performance and compatibility.
- Permission rules and access control should be reviewed after each feature update.

8.2. **Backups**

- User data, tournament records, venue records, and social interactions should be backed up regularly.
- File uploads and public assets should be maintained through proper storage backup procedures.

8.3. **Technical Assistance**

- Contact the system support team or admin through the help section or designated support channel.
- Common issues include the following:

- **Cannot login**: Check email/password or contact admin for account review.
- **Verification pending**: Wait for admin approval or submit supporting documents if required.
- **Venue booking rejected**: Review booking requirements and venue availability.
- **Tournament registration issues**: Confirm eligibility, format rules, and registration status.
- **Livestream unavailable**: Check stream configuration and assigned organizer permissions.
- **Match score updates not saving**: Confirm authorized role and event assignment.

9. **Conclusion**

SportHub is a role-based sports and event management platform designed to support multiple user groups and operations. Each module has a dedicated purpose, and access is controlled through role-based authentication to ensure secure and efficient platform usage.

This manual serves as a guide for developers, administrators, and users to understand how the system works, how each module is used, and how daily operations are carried out successfully.
